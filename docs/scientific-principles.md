# Scientific principles

Use established physics as the baseline and state each hypothesis with a falsifiable prediction and measurable outcome. Apply dimensional checks and conservation laws within the declared model boundaries. Record numerical assumptions, tolerances, input data and versions.

Separate gravitational effects from radiation pressure, electromagnetism, acceleration, vibration, buoyancy and heating. A numerical discrepancy warrants convergence checks, controls and independent verification. Preserve negative findings and failed validation checks. Maintain citations for factual or model-specific claims. Keep speculative physics disabled until its assumptions and predictions can be reviewed independently.
