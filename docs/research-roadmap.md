# Research roadmap

1. Establish trusted numerical models, controls and analytical comparisons for baseline experiments.
2. Extend seeded search with uncertainty estimates and validated performance bounds. Introduce more efficient optimization when parameter sweeps warrant it.
3. Add structured literature review and hypothesis generation with citation verification and independent scientific criticism.
4. Add advanced models and topology search as their independent validation cases become available.
5. Evaluate whether sufficiently measurable hypotheses justify controlled physical replication.

Next work for the existing benchmarks includes a converged off-axis finite-disk solver, independently cross-checked 3D field maps and material-specific uncertainty estimates.
